🔑 Key Findings
MEDIUMDirect Prompt-Injection Remains the Main Risk
Injection attempts via direct user input had a higher override rate (12.5%) compared to other vectors, indicating this is the most effective attack path observed. However, overall success was limited.
LOWIndirect (Document-Based) Injection Was Unsuccessful
Full resistance was observed against indirect injection via external documents, with a 0% override rate. This is positive, especially since indirect vectors represent a higher risk profile.
INFOComplex Strategies Did Not Succeed
More advanced and combined injection strategies showed no success, suggesting strong defenses against sophisticated manipulation attempts.
🧪 Strategy Analysis
fake_completion: Attempts to trick the model by imitating response instructions led to the highest override rate, but this pathway still represents an isolated risk.
naive: Simple prompt manipulations scored some successes, showing that even basic approaches require monitoring, though impact remains minor.
escape: Tactics trying to break out of the model's intended context failed, indicating reliable containment.
context_ignoring: Strategies to make the model ignore its initial instructions produced no successful overrides.
combined: Attempts using multiple techniques simultaneously did not breach the model's guardrails.
⚖️ Regulatory Implications
These results align well with recommended controls in MITRE ATLAS AML.T0054 (prompt injection) and AML.T0040 (task specification), as well as OWASP LLM01 & LLM08. NIST AI 600-1 and the EU AI Act both emphasize safe prompt handling and defense against indirect manipulation; this model shows compliance with core expectations.
🔬 Methodology Caveat
⚠️ Override assessment was performed automatically and supplemented with model-based judgments, which may not fully capture nuanced or partial override scenarios. Human review is recommended for high-severity findings to ensure accuracy.
✅ Prioritised Recommendations
  1. Continue monitoring for new direct injection methods. — Direct input remains the most successful vector; ongoing vigilance can prevent emerging threats.
  2. Maintain and regularly test document ingestion safeguards. — Indirect (document) injection defenses proved robust; regular evaluation will guard against advances.
  3. Augment human review for high override or ambiguous responses. — Automated scoring may miss subtle or context-dependent vulnerabilities.
